\section{Discussion}

To compare the five fitted models we use the likelihood-ratio test (LRT)
together with AIC and BIC. The LRT applies to nested pairs of models: for a
base model with log-likelihood $\ell_0$ and an expanded model with
log-likelihood $\ell_1$ the statistic
\begin{equation}
    \Lambda = 2(\ell_1 - \ell_0)
    \label{eq:lrt_statistic}
\end{equation}
is asymptotically $\chi^2$-distributed with degrees of freedom equal to the
difference in the number of free parameters, under the null that the base
model is adequate. Small $p$-values therefore favour the expanded model.
AIC and BIC do not require nesting; lower values indicate a better
likelihood--complexity trade-off, with BIC penalising parameters more
heavily than AIC.

Table~\ref{tab:model_stats} reports the per-model statistics for all five
models in the hierarchy of Figure~\ref{fig:diagram:model_hierarchy}.
Table~\ref{tab:lrt_comparison} reports the LRT, $p$-value, $\Delta\text{AIC}$
and $\Delta\text{BIC}$ for each edge of the hierarchy, where the difference
is taken as expanded minus base so that negative values favour the expanded
model.
