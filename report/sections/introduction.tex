
\section{Introduction}
Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) are a powerful tool for modeling sequential data, where the underlying states of the system are not directly observable. 
They have been widely used in various fields such as speech recognition, natural language processing, and bioinformatics. \cite{ultralytics_hmm_glossary}. \\
The goal of this project is to inference if a person is in a room based on $\text{CO}_2$ data collected from a sensor in the room. 
To do this, we will use a Hidden Markov Model to model the relationship between the observed $\text{CO}_2$ levels and the hidden states of whether a person is in the room or not and if a window is open or not. 
When training the models, we will use \texttt{JAX} \cite{jax2018github}  and \texttt{EQUINOX} \cite{kidger2021equinox} for automatic differentiation and optimization,
and we will use \texttt{Optax} \cite{optax2020github} as our optimization library. \newline
